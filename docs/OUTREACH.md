# Guided outreach

An administrator can turn on **Enable Outreach for this workspace** under **Settings → Workspace**. Existing workspaces start with the setting off. Once enabled, signed-in workspace members can use **Outreach** in the sidebar or **Start calling** on a call-ready Pipeline opportunity.

1. Pick companies from active sales opportunities in the order you want to call them. To prepare a target, add the company switchboard or a contact number, capture the qualification evidence and select **Start outreach**. The head-office number, website and saved LinkedIn links also appear on Companies, Targets and Pipeline.
2. Choose which saved number to call. Check it against TPS, CTPS and your own do-not-call list before confirming screening in Outreach. You can then click the phone number to open your device’s dialler. [ICO guidance on business-to-business marketing calls](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)
3. Use **Gatekeeper**, **Decision-maker** or **Voicemail** for the HSM App scripts. Expand an objection to see a short reply. **Add to outcome** records the objection you actually heard.
4. Choose an outcome and select **Save & next**. A dated follow-up appears in CRM tasks. **Demo booked** requires an agreed date and time and creates an **Attend booked demo** task. **Do not contact** writes to the selected company or contact. **Skip company** advances the list without writing a call.

The session list and position are kept in the current browser tab. Saved calls and tasks are part of the CRM timeline and survive disabling and re-enabling Outreach. The release uses the existing workspace settings and CRM metadata, so it can run on the current database schema.
