# Guided outreach

An administrator can turn on **Enable Outreach for this workspace** under **Settings → Workspace**. Existing workspaces start with the setting off. Once enabled, signed-in workspace members can use **Outreach** in the sidebar or **Start calling** on a call-ready Pipeline opportunity.

1. Pick companies from active sales opportunities in the order you want to call them. **Review phone details** edits the switchboard and website in the call list. **Website** opens the saved site while **Search the web** helps find missing details. The head-office number, website and saved LinkedIn links also appear on Companies, Targets and Pipeline.
2. Choose which saved number to call. Check it against TPS, CTPS and your own do-not-call list before confirming screening in Outreach. You can then click the phone number to open your device’s dialler. [ICO guidance on business-to-business marketing calls](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)
3. Use **Gatekeeper**, **Decision-maker** or **Voicemail** for the HSM App scripts. Common objections and replies are visible as soon as you open a company. **Add to outcome** records the objection you actually heard. Administrators can use **Edit call guide** in Outreach to update the workspace’s wording.
4. Choose an outcome and select **Save & next**. A dated follow-up appears in CRM tasks. **Demo booked** requires an agreed date and time and creates an **Attend booked demo** task. **Do not contact** writes to the selected company or contact. **Skip company** advances the list without writing a call.

The session list and position are kept in the current browser tab. Saved calls and tasks are part of the CRM timeline and survive disabling and re-enabling Outreach. The release uses the existing workspace settings and CRM metadata, so it can run on the current database schema.

With OpenAI enabled for the workspace, **Draft with AI** proposes wording for a single script or objection reply. Review it, select **Use draft** and save the guide to share the change. **Find head-office number with AI** searches the saved company domain and shows a source alongside any suggestion. Check the source, then choose whether to use and save the number. Screen the number before calling.

Connect Hunter or Voilà Norbert in **Settings → AI & connections → Email finding**. When preparing the call list, **Find work email** looks up the named contact using their full name and the saved company domain. During a call session, it follows the contact whose number is selected, or the primary contact for the switchboard. FreeMax uses the workspace’s provider order and usage caps. A match is saved to the existing contact and **Email [contact]** opens an addressed draft in your mail app. Each lookup starts with a click. Review the recipient and send the draft yourself.
