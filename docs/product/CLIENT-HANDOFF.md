# Won opportunity and client handoff

When an opportunity becomes an agreed engagement, GUD keeps its sales history intact and shows the same record on **Live projects**.

The existing opportunity keeps:

- company and primary stakeholders;
- selected service or product stream;
- agreed scope and expected value;
- its commercial owner;
- promises, constraints and important dates;
- source opportunity and a human-readable activity summary;
- delivery notes for the team taking the work forward.

Live projects adds a delivery stage, next milestone and due date. New and existing wins start in Kickoff until the team chooses a delivery stage. This is a view of the same opportunity, not a copied record or an external project creation. Sales status stays Won. Invoices, support records and external transfers are not created automatically.

Delivery edits are explicit and audited. Archiving hides the record from active views while preserving history. See [Live projects](../LIVE-PROJECTS.md) for the current workflow and supported stages.
