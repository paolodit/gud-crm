# Won opportunity and client handoff

GUD's responsibility ends when a sales opportunity becomes an agreed engagement and the receiving delivery system has enough context to begin safely.

A future handoff record should contain:

- company and primary stakeholders;
- selected service or product stream;
- agreed scope and expected value;
- commercial owner and delivery owner;
- promises, constraints and important dates;
- source opportunity and a human-readable activity summary;
- destination system and transfer status.

Handoff must be explicit and auditable. Marking an opportunity Won should not silently create projects, invoices or support records.
